Crux Nxtion • Design System

ONE SYSTEM. TWO MOODS.

Everything is built from the two colours in the logo. Events (A1) is the dark, nightlife mood. Consultancy and shared pages (A2a) are the light, conversational mood. Same fonts, same buttons, same ticket motif — so the two services always feel like one brand.

BUTTONS — ONE SHAPE

Every button, tab, chip and tag uses the same slanted shape. Primary is filled; secondary is the same shape drawn as an outline; chips and tags step down in size. Slant: 10px large, 8px medium, 6px small. Red is the action colour on Events pages, purple on Consultancy pages. Only text links stay unshaped.

TYPE

Display — Bebas Neue
H1 62–96px, H2 52–60px, H3 28–30px. Always uppercase, tight leading.
Body — Space Grotesk
Descriptions 14–15px, lead paragraphs 16–17px, line-height 1.6–1.8.
Eyebrow
11px, 600, 3px tracking, uppercase, in the accent colour. One per section, above the title.
Numbers
01, 02… in Bebas, accent colour.

RULES

Titles
White on dark, ink on light. Never coloured, except one highlighted phrase.
Descriptions
Muted colour, never pure white or pure black.
Text on photos
Always white, over a dark gradient. Never the accent colour except for chips.
Sections
Each section fills roughly one screen (about 800px). No thin filler bands.
Call-out bar
Sits above the header on every page: logo blue on A1, purple on A2a.
60 · 30 · 10
60% ink and photos, 30% logo blue and its tint (bars, marquee, eyebrows, links), 10% accents split between red (actions) and purple (Consultancy). Colour = meaning: blue is Events, purple is Consultancy, red is “do something”.
Where CTAs go
Hero primary → Contact. Hero secondary → the most useful next page (Events: upcoming events; Consultancy: How it works). “Explore Consultancy” → Consultancy Home. “View all services” → that service line’s Services page. Ticket cards → the single event. Pre-footer: Plan An Event and Talk Business Strategy → Contact.
Motion
Hover lifts on cards, gentle rise on load, floating stickers. Nothing that loops loudly.
