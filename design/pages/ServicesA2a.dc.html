Consultancy Services

FIVE WAYS
WE HELP.

Each one starts with a conversation and ends with a plan you can act on. Pick the one that sounds like your week — or book a call and we'll work out which fits.

01 / START

BUSINESS SETUP & STRATEGY

You might be saying

Starting from scratch and not sure what the business should even look like?

We'll say

We work out the model, the positioning and the operating plan — then a launch plan you can follow.

Every business starts as a rough idea. We help you pressure-test it, decide who it is for, price it sensibly and set up the structure to run it — so launch day feels like the next step, not a leap.

You leave with
A clear business modelPositioning that makes senseA step-by-step launch plan

GOOD FOR  First-time founders, side hustles going full-time, new product lines.

02 / BRAND

BRANDING & MARKETING

You might be saying

You do good work, but your brand doesn't say so.

We'll say

We sharpen the identity and the message, then build a marketing plan that fits your real budget.

Good work deserves a brand that says so. We define your voice, your look and your message, then turn it into a marketing plan sized to your real budget — social, local, partnerships and word of mouth.

You leave with
Identity and messagingA go-to-market planA brand you can brief anyone on

GOOD FOR  Businesses that look smaller than they are, rebrands, new launches.

03 / GROW

BUSINESS GROWTH

You might be saying

Busy, but the business isn't really growing.

We'll say

We find the constraint, design the offer and map a realistic route to more customers and revenue.

Growth is rarely one big idea — it is the right few moves in the right order. We find what is capping your revenue, sharpen the offer and build a simple pipeline you can keep feeding.

You leave with
A growth plan with prioritiesA sharper offerA simple pipeline

GOOD FOR  Established businesses stuck on a plateau.

04 / ACTIVATE

ACTIVATION GROWTH

You might be saying

People know you — they just don't act.

We'll say

We design campaigns and activations that turn an audience into customers, and help you run them.

Some businesses have the audience but not the action. We design campaigns, live activations and partnerships that turn attention into enquiries and sales — drawing on years of getting crowds into rooms.

You leave with
Campaign conceptsLive activation ideasPartnerships to pursue

GOOD FOR  Retail, food, hospitality and community brands.

05 / AUDIT

BUSINESS AUDIT & ADVISORY

You might be saying

You want an honest second opinion.

We'll say

We look at the whole business, tell you what's working and what isn't, and recommend the next move.

An outside view is hard to get from the inside. We audit your offer, brand, numbers and customers, tell you plainly what is working and what is not, and hand you a prioritised action plan.

You leave with
An honest auditWhat to fix firstAn action plan

GOOD FOR  Owners who want a second opinion before the next big decision.
